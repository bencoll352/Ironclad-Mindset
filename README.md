# IRONCLAD MINDSET — Forged in Struggle. Built for Strength.

**Full-Stack Community, Sobriety, Mood-Food Nutrition & B2B Corporate Wellness Progressive Web App (PWA)**

> *"Most wellness platforms are built by people who have never stared at the ceiling at 3 AM, battled a 5 PM craving after a brutal shift, or hid mental health struggles behind a brave face. Ironclad Mindset was founded by a full-time cook and business leader who walked through alcohol dependency and mental health struggles and came out stronger."*

---

## Core Modules

1. **Persistent Global Header & "5 PM SOS / Craving Reset" Red Button (`#E63946`)**
   - Live Sobriety Streak & Money Saved (`£`) counter.
   - 60-Second 4-4-4-4 Box Breathing animation (`framer-motion`), Rapid HALT Check, Play the Tape Forward, and One-Tap Escalation (`Samaritans 116 123`, `CALM 0800 58 58 58`, or Cornerman AI).
2. **Module 1: "The Forge" (Mission Dashboard & Loop-Style Habit Tracker)**
   - Expandable Founder Manifesto & launch script, Sobriety & Resilience Counter, `+1 Day Forged` confetti celebration, 5 Iron Milestone Badges, 28-Day Consistency Heatmap Grid, 3 Vikunja-style Daily Non-Negotiables, and 7-Day Neurochemical Recovery Chart.
3. **Module 2: "The Cornerman" (24/7 AI Agent with Playbook RAG)**
   - Powered by `@google/genai` (Gemini 2.5) + embedded RAG over all 5 Playbooks and 5 Mood-Food Recipes, with clickable RAG Citation Badges and a 3-Ingredient Fridge Dopamine Recipe Generator.
4. **Module 3: "Fuel The Mind" (Mealie-Style Nutrition & £40 Budget Hub)**
   - 5 chef-built gut-brain recovery recipes with full macros, cost per serving (`£`), Brain Benefit callouts, and an interactive **£40 Weekly Aldi/Tesco Supermarket Shopping List**.
5. **Module 4: "The Playbooks" (Interactive Document Vault & Worksheets)**
   - All 5 multi-section guides with Copy Text, Print/Save PDF, Ask Cornerman, live **Interactive HALT & CBT Trigger Worksheet** (Playbook 4), and **DocuSeal Workplace Policy Sign-Off** (Playbook 5).
6. **Module 5: "Lifelines & UK Directory"**
   - One-tap `tel:` UK crisis lines, Hospitality Action, The Lighthouse Club, SMART Recovery UK, Alcohol Change UK, Club Soda, AA, and the **UK Sober Matchday & 0.0% Tap Finder**.
7. **Module 6: "The Locker Room & B2B Portal"**
   - 4-channel UK peer community wall with **Send Iron** fist-bump counter + interactive **B2B Corporate Wellness ROI Calculator**, `n8n` / `Twenty CRM` webhook dispatch, and instant printable Corporate Wellness Proposal generator.
